Introduction

Tianru Zhang is a Postdoctoral Researcher in Department of Information Technology at Uppsala University. He holds a PhD in Scientific Computing from Uppsala University. Previously, he received his MSc degree in Statistics and Data Science from ENSAI (National school of statistics and information analysis of France), and his BSc degree in Mathematics from University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). Before joining Uppsala University, he also worked as an assistant researcher in Fujitsu R&D center, Beijing, and an intern Data Scientist in IRT SystemX, France. Zhang is interested in research fields including Data Management, Federated Machine Learning, and Multimodel LLMs.
